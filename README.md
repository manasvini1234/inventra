# Inventra – Inventory Management System

Inventra is a Smart Inventory and Warehouse Management System designed to digitize stock operations and provide centralized visibility of products, stock levels, warehouses, and inventory movements.

## 🚀 Project Overview

Inventra helps Inventory Managers and Warehouse Staff manage inventory operations digitally instead of relying on manual registers and spreadsheets.

The system covers:

- Product management
- Stock tracking
- Receipts
- Delivery Orders
- Internal Transfers
- Stock Adjustments
- Move History
- Warehouse and Location management
- Inventory dashboard
- Low-stock and out-of-stock monitoring
